Your information

Golf Creator Tour uses the details you submit to manage creator and brand interest in the new season and contact you about relevant opportunities.

What you share

The signup forms request your name, email address, optional social profile or required company details, and your consent to receive GCT season and opportunity emails. Brand interest can also include your selected campaign objective.

How it is used

Creator and brand signups are sent to GCT’s existing waitlist service and stored in its database. The service uses technical request information for abuse prevention. A signup does not create an app account, confirm campaign selection or purchase a service.

Experience inquiries

The experience form prepares an email using the property information you enter. Your email application opens so you can review and send it yourself.

Change your choice

To withdraw your signup or request deletion, email info@golfcreatoropen.com.

Website measurement

This website emits page and action events within the browser without form-field values. This redesign does not add a third-party analytics provider, advertising pixel or tracking cookie. External destinations, including the app and Instagram, have their own data practices.

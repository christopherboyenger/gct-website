FAQ

A few things to know.

Is it free for creators to join GCT?+

Yes. Creators can join the GCT community for free. Review the requirements and terms of each opportunity before taking part.

What can brands do with GCT?+

Work with golf creators on product seeding, brand challenges and content campaigns. Start with your audience, objectives, deliverables and budget, then agree the campaign scope.

How much does a creator campaign cost?+

Campaign costs depend on the creators, deliverables, product, management and usage rights involved. Agree the scope and creator compensation before the work begins.

Can we use creator content in ads?+

Paid use needs the creator’s permission and agreed usage rights. Define the channels, duration and intended use in the campaign brief, including any Meta partnership ads.

Can courses and destinations work with GCT?+

Yes. GCT works with courses, resorts and destinations to plan hosted creator rounds, visits and content experiences. Share your property, timing and goals to start the conversation.

Does paying for a campaign buy Tour points or qualification?+

No. Campaign spend does not purchase points, rankings, Tour Cards or qualification. Participation in competition follows the published sporting rules.

How do I get started?+

Creators can create a profile in the GCT app. Brands can start a campaign in the app or contact GCT to discuss a brief. Courses and destinations can plan a creator visit with the team.

Can I talk to the GCT team first?+

Yes. Use “Talk to GCT” or email info@golfcreatoropen.com. Tell us whether you are a creator, brand, course or destination, and what you have in mind.

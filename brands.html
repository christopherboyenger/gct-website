How It Works

From objective to close report

  1. 01

    Set the objective

    One clear goal per campaign. We translate it into a mechanic, cohort size, compensation and rights package on a single strategy call.

  2. 02

    We build the cohort

    Hand-picked, verified golf creators matched to your audience and objective — with reliability records from real Tour delivery history.

  3. 03

    Launch & manage

    Briefs, contracts, approvals and prefunded payments run in the GCT app. Creators deliver on deadline because their season depends on it.

  4. 04

    Verify & renew

    Scraper-verified views and completion rates — never screenshots. A close report with a renewal recommendation lands within days.

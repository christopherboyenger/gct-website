Build it around your property

  1. Choose the objective

    A course introduction, new experience, content library or a booking campaign with agreed tracking.

  2. Plan the visit

    Discuss creator fit, dates, filming access, hospitality and travel before inviting creators.

  3. Agree on the work

    Set content deliverables, review arrangements, compensation and usage rights.

  4. Host and review

    Deliver the experience, then review the content and agreed measures together.

Clear on both sides

Your team knows the property. Creators bring the perspective. The plan connects the two.

Agree on who covers golf, lodging, meals, travel and creator fees. Build in filming permissions, weather alternatives and the right to use the finished content.

Good questions

Who is this for?

Golf courses, resorts, destinations and operators of golf experiences looking to discuss creator-led content and hosted activations.

Does hosting guarantee bookings?

No. Awareness and bookings are different objectives. If bookings are the goal, discuss tracking links, offers and reporting as part of the plan.

Are these trips for creators to book?

This page is for hosts and operators. Creators interested in opportunities should join through the creator page and review the terms of each experience.

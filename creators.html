Creator FAQ

Does it cost anything to join the Tour?

No. Joining the Tour and competing for points is free, and creators never pay to access brand work. Optional Tour Membership adds identity and access perks — never a pay-to-play advantage.

How do I get paid?

Commissioned briefs carry guaranteed cash for accepted work, and sponsored challenges disclose their rewards up front. Contracts, approvals and payouts all run inside the GCT app.

What size following do I need?

The Tour is built for golf-native creators of every size. Points reward growth, consistency and delivery — not just raw follower counts — so newer creators can climb fast.

How do I qualify for the Golf Creator Open?

Qualification is earned through the season leaderboard. The highest-ranked creators at the qualification deadline receive invites to the annual Tour Championship.

Which platforms count for points?

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube connect directly in the app. Verified growth and engagement across connected channels feed your season points automatically.

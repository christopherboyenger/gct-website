FAQ

Questions creators ask.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creators join the Golf Creator Tour free.

Who can join?

Golf creators with a public social account who are actively posting and have 1,000+ followers.

How do I earn money?

Brands post challenges and campaigns in the GCT app. Each one shows the brief, product offer and payment terms before you join, and you’re paid through GCT according to those terms.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Creators with 10,000 or more followers qualify for a place on the season leaderboard and a Tour member card. Rank through the season to work toward the Golf Creator Open.

What is the Golf Creator Open?

The annual championship of the Golf Creator Tour: creators, brands, competition, content and community together in one weekend. Creators qualify through the season leaderboard under the published qualification rules.

When does the season start?

The 2027 season starts November 1, 2026. Sign up now to be ready from the first day.

Good questions

Does it cost to join?

Joining GCT is free. You do not need a paid Tour membership to express interest in the new season.

Do I need a huge audience?

Bring your golf content and your perspective. Eligibility and selection can differ by opportunity, so review each brief rather than assuming one follower count applies to everything.

Is every opportunity paid?

Opportunities may involve paid content, gifted products or hosted experiences. Check the compensation, expenses and deliverables for each before accepting. Joining does not guarantee work or earnings.

What happens after I sign up?

You’ll be on the creator waitlist for the next season. We’ll contact you using the email you provide. This form does not create an app account or approve you for a campaign.

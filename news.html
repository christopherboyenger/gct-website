Why this exists

Turn attention into intelligence.

Golf creator business news is fragmented across social feeds, press releases, brand channels, podcasts and traditional media. GCT Daily is built to collect the signal, verify it, add context and make it useful.

The bigger opportunity is what happens behind the article: each story can also identify active brands, growing creators, decision-makers, sponsorship activity and partnership opportunities for the GCT ecosystem.

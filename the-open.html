Three Days at PGA National

What happens at the Open

  1. 01

    Championship golf

    Qualified creators compete for the season title and the championship purse — real golf, real stakes, filmed and clipped all weekend.

  2. 02

    Content everywhere

    Every hole is a set. Creators produce a season's worth of content in three days, with sponsor products and activations in frame.

  3. 03

    Creator-brand networking

    Lounges, dinners and hosted hospitality connect brands with the exact creators they'll work with next season.

  4. 04

    Sponsor activations

    On-course tents, product showcases, closest-to-the-pin moments and gifting suites — owned moments with defined deliverables.

Pricing FAQ

What does the subscription actually cover?

Platform access, creator cohort curation, campaign setup and management, contracts and approvals, verified measurement and your campaign close report. Creator payouts are budgeted separately per campaign.

How are creators paid?

Each campaign carries a prefunded creator budget. Commissioned work is guaranteed cash on acceptance; challenge rewards are disclosed up front. Funds release when you approve delivery.

What counts as an "active" campaign?

Any campaign in draft or open status. Close a campaign and the slot frees up immediately — plans gate on how many campaigns run at once, not how many you run per year.

Can we start small and upgrade?

Yes. Most brands start on Seeding to prove the channel, then move to Growth once two or three objectives are running. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

How is performance verified?

Views and engagement are scraper-verified against the live posts — not screenshots — and reports keep verified, pending and estimated metrics clearly separated.

Do you offer event sponsorship instead of a subscription?

Yes — the Golf Creator Open, content days and lounges are sold as sponsorship packages with defined deliverables and rights. Book a call and we'll match the format to your goal.

Not sure which plan fits?

Bring your objective to a 30-minute strategy call and we'll scope the right plan, cohort and budget — no obligation.

Plan a campaign

Good questions

What can a campaign include?

Discuss creator-made content, product seeding and longer-term creator partnerships. The agreed brief defines what will be created, where it will appear and which creators are involved.

What will it cost?

Costs depend on the scope, creator compensation, product logistics and content usage. Agree on the campaign budget and any GCT fees before committing. Sending your interest does not purchase a campaign.

Can we use the content in ads?

Paid advertising rights must be agreed with the creator. Specify platforms, duration, territory, editing and any exclusivity. Organic posting does not automatically grant an unlimited advertising license.

Can you guarantee sales?

No. Set measurable objectives and agree on the available reporting. Content volume, audience size, views and attributed sales are different measures and should be assessed separately.

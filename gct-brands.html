FAQ

Questions brands ask.

How do creators find my campaign?

Creators discover your campaign in the GCT app and choose opportunities that fit their content and audience.

Who sets the terms?

You do. Set your goals, deliverables, creator compensation and content usage rights before the campaign begins.

Can I use creator content in ads?

With creator permission and agreed usage rights, selected content can support Meta partnership ads.

How are creators paid?

Creators are paid through GCT according to the agreed terms once you approve the agreed deliverables.

What does GCT cost?

Starter has no subscription and adds a 12% GCT service fee to each paid collaboration, with a $25 minimum per collaboration. Pro is $299 a month, or $2,988 billed annually, and lowers the fee to 7% with no minimum. Managed campaigns are quoted separately.

Does the creator receive their full rate?

Yes. Creators receive their agreed fee. GCT’s platform fee is added to the brand’s total.

What does a hire cost?

On Starter, a $500 hire costs $560: $500 to the creator plus a $60 fee (12%). A $100 hire costs $125, because the $25 minimum fee applies instead of $12. On Pro, the same $500 hire costs $535 ($500 plus a $35 fee), with the Pro subscription billed separately.

Can Starter launch campaigns?

No. Open and invite-only campaigns are part of Pro, with up to 3 active at a time. A Pro campaign with 10 creators at $250 each comes to $2,675: $2,500 to the creators plus a $175 fee. Starter is for direct hires: up to 2 new hires per calendar month and 1 active paid collaboration at a time.

When is Pro worth it?

On fees alone, Pro’s 7% rate covers its $299 monthly subscription once you pay creators more than about $5,980 a month through GCT. On the annual plan, $2,988 billed annually (equivalent to $249 a month), that point is about $4,980 a month. Whatever you spend, campaigns and more than 2 new hires a month need Pro.

Do gifts require posting?

No. Product gifting is voluntary: creators aren’t required to post, and gifting isn’t charged a GCT fee. Gifting campaigns run on Pro. If you need content, agree it with the creator as a paid collaboration.

Can we activate at events?

Yes. Brands can plan activations at the Golf Creator Open and GCT creator socials. Get in touch to plan 2027 activations.

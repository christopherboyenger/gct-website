About Us

One network. One season.
One championship.

Golf Creator Tour is the operating network for creator-led golf marketing — the place where brands run their golf creator strategy, and verified creators build careers and competitive status all season long.

What We Believe

Built on three convictions

Creators deserve a season

Influencer marketing treats creators like inventory. A tour gives them stakes: points, streaks, rank and a championship worth chasing — which makes their content, and their delivery, better.

Brands deserve proof

Screenshots aren't measurement. Every campaign on GCT closes with scraper-verified views and completion rates, with verified, pending and estimated numbers kept clearly separate.

Golf deserves its own network

Generic creator marketplaces don't understand tee sheets, sim bays or launch monitors. GCT is golf-native end to end — from the challenge briefs to the Open itself.

The Ecosystem

How it all fits together

  1. 01

    The App

    Creators earn points, take on challenges and paid briefs, track the leaderboard and manage payouts — all in the GCT app.

  2. 02

    The Season

    A year-long points race across social growth, engagement, challenges, matches and events, with a public live leaderboard.

  3. 03

    The Brands

    Campaigns plug into the season: sponsored challenges, commissioned cohorts and event activations with verified results.

  4. 04

    The Open

    The annual Tour Championship at PGA National — where the season's best creators and the Tour's brand partners come together.
